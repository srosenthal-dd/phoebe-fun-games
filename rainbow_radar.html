🌈 Rainbow Radar

Rainbows move every turn — track them, predict where they go, and capture them all!

📋 How to Play

  1. 🎯 Place your rainbows on the grid (3-cell lines, horizontal or vertical)
  2. 👀 Scout — both players see all rainbow positions & directions for 5 seconds
  3. 🔄 Each turn rainbows move 1 cell in their direction (25% chance to turn!) — they bounce off walls. Damaged rainbows can't move!
  4. 🎯 Guess where a rainbow is — hit and you go again! Miss and it's the other player's turn. Re-guess missed cells (rainbows may have moved there!)
  5. 🌡️ Miss → get a proximity hint (🔥 hot / ❄️ cold)
  6. 🏆 Win by capturing all opponent's rainbow cells — or most points after 15 rounds each!
